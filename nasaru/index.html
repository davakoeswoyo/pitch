Nasaru Child Foundation (NCF) · prototypes

Marina picked this design and asked to keep its theme and colours. Two versions of it below. The other two prototypes have been dropped.

Version 2 keeps the same palette and typefaces, and rebuilds the sections, layout, buttons and text around Marina’s answers: the real story of how Nasaru started, why children here start school late, the team and board, what volunteers can actually do, and the real figures as they stand today.

Photos and the NCF logo are now the organisation’s own files rather than borrowed from the old site. Highlighted items are the last few things still needed from Marina.
