Nasaru Child Foundation · choose a design

Three one page designs. Same structure, same content, same photos, three different feels. Click one to view it full size.

All three follow the redesign brief: the eleven section homepage structure, honest early stage positioning, no invented impact numbers, a permanent Current Priority section around the WhyDonate campaign closing 15 September 2026, an expanded Get Involved section with a volunteer form, an Our Journey timeline, and an Updates feed Marina can post to. Built from stock WordPress blocks in the standard editor on the Twenty Twenty-Five theme. No custom code, nothing to maintain. Highlighted items are placeholders the client still needs to supply.
