About Us

Nasaru is at the beginning of its journey. We are building the foundation for a long term early childhood education project in the Maasai community, and we are looking for volunteers, partners and supporters who want to help us build it.

Nasaru Child Foundation is a community led nonprofit organization based in Oltukai Village, Monduli District, Arusha Region, Tanzania. We are committed to improving the lives of vulnerable children through quality early childhood education, child protection, and community development.

In underserved Maasai communities, access to quality education is often limited, and lessons happen wherever there is shade or space. Early childhood is when the habits of learning are formed, which is why we have chosen to start here. We believe that every child deserves a safe place to learn, grow, and reach their full potential.

Working together with families, local leaders, volunteers, and partners, we provide a nurturing learning environment while promoting children’s health, nutrition, and overall well being.

Our Work

Six programmes, one goal

01Early Childhood Education

Providing quality early learning for Maasai children through play based education, literacy, numeracy, and a safe, nurturing environment that prepares them for primary school.

02Safe Preschool Construction

Building permanent classrooms, child friendly toilets, and a secure learning environment where every child can learn safely and with dignity.

03School Feeding Program

Providing nutritious daily meals to improve children’s health, school attendance, and ability to learn.

04Community Engagement

Working with parents, local leaders, and volunteers to strengthen education and create lasting community support for children.

05Learning Resources

Supplying books, educational toys, classroom furniture, and teaching materials that help children thrive.

06Teacher Development

Equipping teachers with modern early childhood education skills to deliver high quality learning experiences.
